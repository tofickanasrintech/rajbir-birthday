05 · a letter for you

To my birthday boy, ❤️

Happy Birthdayyy, honey! 🥹❤️

First of all, I'm really glad you were born. Otherwise, who would I annoy every day? And who would annoy me back? 😭

But seriously, I'm really happy that I have you. You've become such an important part of my life, and I love all the random little moments we have together. The stupid jokes, random arguments, laughing and arguing over absolutely nothing, and all the times we just sit together doing our own thing. Somehow, those are my favourite moments.

I love how comfortable I am with you. I can be completely stupid around you, say whatever comes to my mind, and just be myself. And somehow you still haven't gotten tired of me yet, so I guess you're stuck with me now. 🤭

I love how passionate you are and how seriously you take the things you care about, especially your life and us. You make me feel so safe and secure with you. No wonder I'm so sure about you and can't imagine my life without you.

I really hope and wish that we end up together forever, no matter what. I'll make sure to do my part, and we'll make sure we get there together. ❤️

And remember, my darling, I'll always be with you, even if you don't succeed. I know you will, for sure, but I'm saying this just to remind you that I'll never leave your side. Whether things go perfectly or not, I'll always be here to support you, believe in you, and be with you.

I hope you achieve everything you want and become everything you dream of becoming. I'll always be there to support you, celebrate your wins, listen to your nonsense, and bother you whenever you need it. 😭

Thank you for being my person, for making me laugh, and for making even ordinary days feel a little more special.

I love you loads, birthday boy. ❤️
